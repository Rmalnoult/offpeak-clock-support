Offpeak Clock support

Enter the low-rate hours shown on your electricity plan. Add an appliance and its cycle duration to see the earliest start that keeps the full cycle within those hours.

Frequently asked questions

My plan changes by season. What should I do?
Update the weekly hours in the app when your provider changes them.

Why is there no available start?
No complete low-rate interval within the next two weeks fits the cycle duration. Check your hours and the cycle length.

Will the app start my appliance?
No. It calculates a suggested start time. You start the appliance yourself.

Need help? Email romain.gabin@tctb.fr. Include your iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send account or payment details.
