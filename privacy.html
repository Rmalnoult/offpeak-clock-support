Offpeak Clock privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Offpeak Clock stores your low-rate hours, home time zone and appliance names and durations on your device. The app and widget share these settings locally through an Apple App Group. No account or server is used. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

Offpeak Clock does not collect or transmit your settings, device identifiers or usage activity. It does not track you across apps or websites. You can remove the settings by deleting the app.

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to answer your request.

Contact: contact@tctb.dev.
